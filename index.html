Abstract

Language models lose accuracy when a prompt contains non-evidential context: irrelevant statements from unrelated domains, or random off-distribution tokens, with no competing answer and no semantic overlap with the query. Such noise pervades retrieval-augmented, tool-using, and long-context deployments, yet the failure has lacked a mechanistic account. We trace the corruption to the Intrusion Pathway, which starts with MLPs enriching both query-subject and irrelevant-token representations, parroting heads carry prefix-token content to the prediction position, and relational readers largely avoid unrelated sources. Nevertheless, preserved subject enrichment and selective reading do not prevent intrusion in the broader computation. We then localize part of this interference to a sparse set of attention heads, which we call intrusion heads. Their prefix-derived writes promote distractor-related content, while their reduced support for the correct answer arises primarily from weaker non-prefix contributions rather than direct suppression by the prefix. The same heads transfer across tasks and distractor types and are largely weight-disjoint from the heads that integrate relevant contextual evidence, enabling a class-conditional intervention. Replacing the corrupted last-token residual with its clean counterpart, an oracle probe that requires a matched clean forward pass, recovers 97.6% on average of the distractor-induced drop across four downstream tasks. A deployable mask level knockout of about ten heads, roughly 1% of the model's attention heads, recovers 57% at bounded cost to helpful context.

02 · MLP ENRICHMENT · §3

Germany is still enriched

Subject-position MLP writes make Germany's attributes available at the Germany position. Under the irrelevant prefix that signal is effectively unchanged (App. B.1.1).

The MLPs enrich the irrelevant tokens too. “Computers” picks up its own associations (B.1.2). The two tokens differ in relevance, not in whether they carry associated information.

WATCH The diamonds on the two leftmost slabs. Germany's looks the same in both runs; the prefix diamonds are new. The lost support for Berlin has to happen further along.
03 · PARROTING · §2–3

Prefix tokens get copied to END

Parroting heads carry prefix-token identity into END without a matching induction cue. On a multi-token prefix their attention spreads over several content words, and the write is approximately an attention-weighted mixture of those tokens' unembedding directions.

Swapping the value at one source position with attention held fixed shifts these heads' writes toward the swapped token (B.2.1). The effect is in OV, not QK routing.

04 · RELATIONAL READERS · §3

The readers look away from the filler

Mid-layer readers, selected on golden context, attend to candidates of the expected answer type and assign little attention to unrelated filler (B.4).

WATCH Under the irrelevant prefix the hollow-circle heads barely touch it. Under Golden or Counterfactual they jump to Berlin or Munich. Selective reading does not clean END, which is already tinted by earlier layers.
05 · MIDDLE-BLOCK INTERVENTION · §3

Effective, not selective

Replace every middle-block head's END write with its context-free counterpart while the prefix stays in the input. This restores most of the gold-logit loss. Masking the same heads' END→prefix reads barely helps.

WATCH Switch to Golden with the restore on: most of the evidence benefit goes with it, and it needs a clean donor run.

08 · INTRUSION VS RETRIEVAL · §4

Two populations, mostly apart

Retrieval heads read the answer span in golden context and write toward it. Ranked independently, the top heads of the two populations differ, with a small overlap (Fig. 6). That separation is what makes a class-conditional mask possible.

09 · ORACLE PATCH AND λ · §4–5

The oracle and the deployable mask

The oracle swaps the END residual at the intrusion-band layer for its clean counterpart, so it needs a matched clean pass. The mask needs nothing at inference.

The λ slider under the model interpolates the intrusion heads' writes toward their context-free values: o(λ) = oirr + λ(onctx − oirr).
